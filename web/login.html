Research Prototype

Detect account takeover after login by watching behavior, not passwords.

This Flask prototype captures login typing dynamics, mouse trajectory, click hesitation, page dwell time, and cross-page navigation before scoring each session with rolling-window anomaly detection.

Behavioral signals

Keystroke intervals, key hold time, mouse movement variance, and dwell time per page.

Research control

Replay normal baseline or BOT, HUMAN, and FAST attacker traces from the analyst dashboard.

Adaptive response

Sessions can stay low risk, be monitored, or trigger MFA when the pattern becomes suspicious.
